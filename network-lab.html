Model scope and inbound assumptions

Both CE routers attach directly to the shared client LAN and advertise its selected prefix. VRRP controls only the client outbound gateway. Inbound traffic enters through the selected ISP and follows that ISP’s shortest eligible AS path to either CE, including the VRRP backup. Cloud links represent the Internet boundary; no provider transit is simulated. External policies may override prepend.

These changes affect the simulation only. Generated design configurations remain unchanged.

Cisco · Local Preference and AS-path traffic engineering

Synthetic VRRP: a failed active CE is replaced by the healthy backup; a restored preferred CE becomes active again. WAN failures do not change VRRP roles. No timers, LAN failure, interface tracking or VRRP configuration are generated.
