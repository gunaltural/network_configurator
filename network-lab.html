Model scope and inbound assumptions

Both CE routers attach directly to the shared client LAN and advertise its selected prefix. VRRP controls only the client outbound gateway. Inbound traffic starts at the Internet cloud. The model automatically selects the shortest available AS path across eligible ISP-to-CE connections, including entry through the VRRP backup. Failed devices and links are excluded and the path is recalculated. Equal upstream policies are assumed; ties use connection ID for a stable illustration. This synthetic observer does not simulate actual Internet routing or provider transit. Real upstream policies can override prepend.

These changes affect the simulation only. Generated design configurations remain unchanged.

Cisco · Local Preference and AS-path traffic engineering

Synthetic VRRP: a failed active CE is replaced by the healthy backup; a restored preferred CE becomes active again. WAN failures do not change VRRP roles. No timers, LAN failure, interface tracking or VRRP configuration are generated.
